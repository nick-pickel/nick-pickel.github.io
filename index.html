About Me

I am an Operations & Supply Chain student at the University of Utah's David Eccles School of Business with a strong interest in entrepreneurship, business operations, and technology. I enjoy working through practical business problems and finding ways to make processes more efficient. Through my professional and academic experience, I have worked with Excel, data analysis, presentations, research, real estate information, and business documentation. Outside of school and work, I have pursued several automotive-related ventures, which helped develop my interest in entrepreneurship. I am also interested in how information systems, data, and other business tools can be used to support better decision-making and improve the way organizations operate. In the future, I hope to use my experience in operations and entrepreneurship to eventually own and operate a business of my own.
